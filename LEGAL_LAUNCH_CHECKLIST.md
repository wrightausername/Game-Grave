# Game Grave legal launch gates (owner checklist)

**This is a preparation checklist, not legal advice or a warranty of legal protection.**
The in-app Settings > About legal notice is informational. It is not an
enforceable standalone contract merely because a user can scroll through it.

## Must complete before public sale or wide beta

- [ ] Have a qualified software/consumer-protection attorney identify the
  correct legal entity, governing law and distribution jurisdictions and review
  all end-user provisions. Avoid broad claims of “99% protected” or “zero risk.”
- [ ] Publish an actual End User License Agreement (EULA), with version/effective
  date, party names, scope, refund/warranty disclosures as applicable,
  licensing/install/device rules, termination, dispute terms, contact details,
  and legally compliant warranty/liability restrictions.
- [ ] Obtain demonstrable assent to the EULA at an appropriate step *before*
  use/install or purchase, as advised by counsel. Record version and acceptance
  without unnecessarily collecting identifying information. Do not imply that
  opening the About tab constitutes consent.
- [ ] Publish a separate, accessible Privacy Notice describing what is collected,
  used, stored and transferred (including on-demand Steam cover art, Patch Watch,
  optional analytics/AI, purchases, licensing and device activations), service
  providers, choices/opt-outs, security, retention, deletion rights and contact.
  The text must match actual behavior and be updated when services change.
- [ ] Put short, prominent, task-specific disclosures at risky actions:
  scan limitations, file deletion/recycling, screenshot review, backups,
  restore/overwrite, external game/news requests, and optional AI installation.
  A buried About disclaimer cannot replace those notices.
- [ ] Have the merchant-of-record, refund rules, sales taxes and chargeback
  procedure contractually defined. Ensure “lifetime” Pro claims have a clear,
  consistent meaning in checkout, EULA, website and in-app UI.
- [ ] Clear third-party usage rights for logos, game art, API endpoints and
  marketplace branding. A trademark disclaimer does not license copyrighted art.
- [ ] Complete independent security/privacy review, code signing, reliable
  update verification and backup/restore tests, including unusual failures.
- [ ] Measure FPS and 1%-low impact on real Windows hardware; do not claim
  “zero FPS impact” without substantiation. Release only after agreed tests pass.
- [ ] Confirm user support contact and business identity are available and
  reflect the actual operating entity. Do not invent an address in legal copy.
- [ ] Document per-version tests and preserve copies of policies that were
  actually shown at installation/purchase.

## Non-waivable rights

Consumer protection, unfair/deceptive practices, privacy law, product liability,
and some warranties and remedies cannot be excluded merely through a notice.
Counsel should tailor the enforceable terms for markets where Game Grave ships.
