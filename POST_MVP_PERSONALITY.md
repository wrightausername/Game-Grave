# Post-MVP: Personality and Easter Eggs (Deferred)

**Priority:** Only after game and mod discovery, Graveyard, protected cleanup, undo, and Windows QA have reached acceptance.

Desired tone: quirky, witty, affectionate gamer personality; not excessive or distracting.

Ideas for the later polish phase:
- Occasional unobtrusive retro gaming easter eggs, respectful of trademarks and copyrights.
- Optional lighthearted messages when the app finds neglected games or reclaims storage.
- Hidden achievement-style milestones that do not encourage unsafe deleting.
- Rare, accessible pixel-art animations with reduced-motion support and an off switch.
- A playful mascot or icon variation that NEVER interferes with file selection, warnings, or confirmation.

Do not introduce jokes into consent dialogs, warnings, detection evidence, recovery, or errors.
No sound autoplay, prank deletions, fake scan results, or obscured safety messaging.

This document records the approved future idea; **none of these Easter eggs is implemented yet**.
